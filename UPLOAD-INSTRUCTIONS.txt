SIDELINEIQ v0.15.14 — BAD SNAP + FUMBLE RECOVERY + POSSESSION

BAD SNAP
- Available under BOTH Run and Pass.
- Tap Bad Snap to open the snap details.
- Enter Center #.
- Choose Caught or Not Caught.
- Not Caught is treated as a center fumble.
- If Not Caught, choose Who Recovered: Offense or Defense.
- Defensive recovery produces a turnover.
- Saved play data stores the center, caught/not-caught status, and recovery team.
- Recent Plays describes BAD SNAP / CENTER FUMBLE / recovery.

FUMBLE WORKFLOW
- Removed the browser OK / Cancel confirmation.
- Clicking Fumble now asks Who recovered the loose ball?
- Buttons are Offense and Defense.
- The selected recovery team drives turnover logic and is stored with the play.

POSSESSION
- A football icon now appears beside the team name at the top for the team currently in possession.

PRESERVED

- Kickoff and punt return TDs.
- Sack attribution (#NN Sack).
- 2-point conversion, player memory, and penalty workflows.
