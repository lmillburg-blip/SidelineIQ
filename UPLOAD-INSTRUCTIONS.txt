SIDELINEIQ v0.41.4 — MOCKUP-ACCURATE COMPACT MOBILE HEADER

Root cause found in v0.41.3:
The old <=760px stylesheet still forced .game-state to grid-column:1/-1 and order:-1.
v0.41.3 changed the parent grid but never explicitly reset those child grid placements.
That is why Q1/KICKOFF remained a full-width row and the status controls became a
third column beside the score cards.

v0.41.4 explicitly places:
ROW 1: Team score | Game state | Opponent score
ROW 2: LOS | Ball At | Distance | Down | State
ROW 3: Plays | Undo | Edit | Control | Stats

Target in-app header height: ~136px before borders/gaps.

Also:
- equal-width team score areas
- compact two-line team names
- all five status cells forced into the same row
- LOS controls compressed to fit without horizontal overflow
- navigation reduced to 40px height
- v0.41.3 three-column play workspace retained
- v0.41.3 data migrates forward
