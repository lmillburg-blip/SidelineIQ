SIDELINEIQ v0.40.1 — MOBILE FIELD-CENTERED GAMEPLAY

- Desktop gameplay preserved from v0.30.1.
- Portrait mobile uses a vertical field as the center spine.
- Offense + Special Teams sit left of the field.
- Defense + Penalty sit right of the field.
- Offense/Defense panels can remain open while the field stays visible.
- Pass workflow remains inside Offense with QB, receiver, result, sack and interception branches.
- Mobile top actions now include first-class Plays, Undo, Edit, Analytics.
- Edit defaults directly to the most recent play when no play has been selected.
- All Plays rows are tap-to-edit on mobile in addition to their Edit buttons.
- Field dragging is vertical in portrait mobile; desktop remains horizontal.
- Mobile play log/editor reflow to phone width.
- Existing v0.30.1 analytics reliability fix retained.
- Existing v0.30 workflow-completeness features retained.
- Migrates data from v0.30.1 and earlier.
