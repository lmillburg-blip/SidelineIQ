SIDELINEIQ v0.23.0 — MOBILE LIVE GAME REDESIGN

MOBILE GAMEPLAY
- Period, both team scores, and core app buttons are now in one compact sticky top row.
- The large SidelineIQ brand block is removed from the live-game screen on phone-sized displays.
- Field height reduced to 105px on mobile (about one-third of desktop).
- LOS, Ball At, Distance, Down, and State all sit in one horizontal row.
- Recent Plays panel is hidden on mobile.
- New Plays button opens the complete play log.
- Offense, Defense, Special Teams, and Penalty are collapsible entry windows.
- One entry window can be opened/minimized at a time.
- Special Teams opens automatically when a kickoff or punt is pending.
- The selected window is preserved through normal live-game rerenders.
- Save Play bar is sticky at the bottom of the mobile screen.

DESKTOP
- Existing desktop field, Recent Plays panel, four-column workbench, and game layout remain intact.
- Added Plays button to the top navigation for direct access to the full play log.

- Migrates from v0.22.2
