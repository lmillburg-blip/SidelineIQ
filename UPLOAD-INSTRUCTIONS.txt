SIDELINEIQ v0.15.4 — PENALTY ENFORCEMENT WORKFLOW FIX

Key changes:
- Penalties now move the football even when the underlying play has no yardage entered.
- "Negate play stats" is clarified as:
  "Ignore play yardage; enforce from previous LOS."
- If that option is selected, enforcement starts from the pre-play line of scrimmage.
- Repeat Down / No Play now also enforces from the previous LOS and keeps the same down.
- The yellow first-down line stays at the original line-to-gain unless an automatic/new first down is awarded.
- Accepted live-ball penalties without Repeat Down count the down.
- Declined penalties are recorded but do not affect the ball.

Smart defaults added:
- Holding: 10 yards, Offense, previous-LOS enforcement selected.
- False Start: 5 yards, Offense, previous LOS + repeat down/no play.
- Delay of Game: 5 yards, Offense, previous LOS + repeat down/no play.
- Offside: 5 yards, Defense, previous LOS + repeat down/no play.
- Illegal Formation: 5 yards, Offense, previous-LOS enforcement selected.
- Illegal Motion: 5 yards, Offense, previous-LOS enforcement selected.

Examples:
- Offensive holding with no play yardage entered: ball moves 10 yards backward from previous LOS.
- Defensive offside: ball moves 5 yards forward from previous LOS and the down repeats.
- A former-play penalty uses the same previous-LOS enforcement logic.

Deployment verification:
- Build badge should read v0.15.4.
- JS/CSS and service-worker caches are bumped.
