SIDELINEIQ v0.42.3

Mobile/UI
- Significantly reduced offense and defense icon-button size.
- Increased center-field emphasis.
- Restored/refined polished Analytics and Player Stats presentation.
- Analytics modal remains independently scrollable on mobile.

Penalties
- Added Apply To: Next Play.
- Next-play penalties persist as game state rather than being tied to the current play.
- Supports PAT/2PT fouls enforced on the ensuing kickoff.
- Supports succeeding-spot penalties across quarter/half boundaries.
- Halftime next-play penalties are enforced on the second-half kickoff.
- Pending next-play penalties are visibly summarized in the Penalty pane.

Stats
- TFL remains removed as a manual live-entry action.
- Negative-yardage plays now derive TFL/TFL yards for recorded tacklers.
- Hurry and Missed remain removed from live entry and the main defensive analytics table.

Existing v0.42.2 data migrates forward.
