SIDELINEIQ v0.15.7 — 2-POINT CONVERSION WORKFLOW

Changes:
- "2-Point" is now "2-Point Try."
- Selecting it starts a dedicated conversion workflow.
- Ball automatically moves to OPP 5.
- Run / Pass entry remains available, including remembered QB/RB/WR buttons.
- Try result is REQUIRED:
  * Successful
  * Failed
- Successful adds 2 points.
- Failed adds 0 points.
- The result is saved as its own 2PT play in Recent Plays.
- After either result, the game moves into the kickoff workflow.

Deployment:
- Build badge should read v0.15.7.
- Migrates from v0.15.6.
- JS/CSS and service-worker caches are bumped.
