SIDELINEIQ v0.15.5 — PENALTY PLAYER + FOUL SPOT

New penalty fields:
- Player # field for the player the foul was called on.
- When "Enforce from spot of foul" is checked, a Spot of Foul section appears:
  * OWN / OPP / 50
  * yard line
- The entered foul spot is now the actual enforcement base for both:
  * penalties on the current play
  * penalties applied to the former play
- Penalty summaries include the player number and foul spot.

Examples:
- Holding on #72 at OWN 38, 10 yards: enforcement begins at OWN 38.
- Defensive foul at OPP 42, 5 yards: enforcement begins at OPP 42.
- If "Enforce from spot of foul" is not selected, the existing previous-LOS / normal enforcement rules still apply.

Deployment verification:
- Build badge should read v0.15.5.
- JS/CSS and service-worker cache versions are bumped.
